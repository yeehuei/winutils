# 07 — Twelve-week schedule

About **6 hours a week**, 72 hours in total: about **53 hours of exercises** and about **19 hours of reading**. If a week runs over, move the rest to the next week and push everything back. Do not skip exercises to catch up.

Each week ends with a **deliverable**: something written, saved and dated in your hypothesis log or working folder. If you cannot produce it, the week is not done.

Times are guides. A first-time Python install can take an evening on its own.

## After week 12

- If your real-fund rule **failed** its pre-registered holdout: close it. Pick a new hypothesis from your log. A new idea with a mechanism is better than a variant.
- If it **passed**: run the paper plan. That takes months, not weeks: at least the trade count and the period you set in the plan. Write a decision memo at the end. Live trading is still a separate decision, made only with a paper record in hand, and outside this plan.
- Either way, keep the weekly habit: about one hour reading, the rest doing.

## Weekly time check

At the end of each week, write two numbers in your log: hours reading, hours doing. If reading is more than a third of the total two weeks running, cut reading, not doing.
